Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Valera Discord Bot collects, uses, and protects your information when you interact with it. Your privacy is important, and we do our best to keep your data safe and minimal.

What We Collect

  • Discord User ID, username or nickname, and statistics (XP, points, leaderboard positions) needed to provide Valera's features.
  • No sensitive information (such as passwords, emails, or direct messages) is collected or stored by Valera.
  • Server configuration data if administrators customize Valera's settings.

How We Use Data

  • To create and maintain server leaderboards, XP systems, and other features provided by Valera.
  • For moderation tools, such as warnings, mutes, or kicks, if Valera provides these functionalities.
  • To improve and monitor Valera’s performance and stability.

Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored securely and only as long as necessary for Valera's operation.
  • Access to Valera's data is restricted to authorized administrators/developers only.
  • Your data will never be shared, sold, or transferred to third parties except as required by law.

Your Rights

  • You may request deletion of your stored data by contacting the server administrators.
  • Contact us if you have questions or concerns about privacy related to Valera.
